Packet 53 made the final bounded height-evidence pass for PRJ-1111087 without reopening the Packet 51 rule profile or Packet 52A FAR work. The private Fourth CD plan remains unpublished.

The plan provides materially useful but incomplete height evidence. T-1 states a project maximum of `40'-0\"`. C-1 provides existing topography and spot elevations. C002 provides proposed finish-floor, pad, contour, and spot-grade elevations. A0.2/A0.3 and the roof plans identify seven building modules, setbacks, building edges, and flat built-up roofs. A2.1/A2.2 and A3-0 through A3-4 show story stacks, parapets, selected grade lines, and direct architectural elevation dimensions, including representative dimensions for Buildings 1 and 5.

Those facts do not seal a San Diego Municipal Code §113.0270 height. The project-data maximum does not identify a controlling structure or datum. The architectural dimensions do not prove the lower of existing or proposed grade directly below every roof/parapet top point, the complete lowest-grade search within five feet, absolute highest-point elevations, or the combined measurement treatment of modules separated by less than six feet. They are retained as architectural evidence and are not silently relabeled as Code heights.

Footnote 18 remains separately unresolved. The plans show side property/setback lines, roof edges, and portions above 30 feet, but they do not coordinate a dimensioned 60-degree plane from both side setback lines with each controlling roof/parapet point using the Code datum. A 40-foot maximum does not establish angled-plane compliance.

No bounded City review artifact explicitly confirms or questions height, grade, Footnote 18, or Coastal Height Limit treatment. Silence remains non-evidence.

The height research loop is closed unless external certified evidence is supplied.
