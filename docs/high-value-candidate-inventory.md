## Density and lot-area semantics to preserve

The 21 RS/RX/RT density cells state one dwelling unit per lot. The 12 RM cells express square feet of lot area per dwelling unit, with Table 131-04G footnotes 1 and 2; RM-5-12 also has footnote 36 concerning named community plan areas and the dwelling-unit/guest-room mechanism. These mechanisms are independent of zone digits. A separate minimum-lot-area table value is not a substitute density denominator. The inventory performs no parcel calculation.

Minimum-lot-area purpose must be resolved from the development-regulation and legal-lot context; it must not be presumed to be solely a subdivision threshold. RX/RT also require the bounded alley credit in §131.0441. Final semantics belong to the authority closure, not this selection step.
