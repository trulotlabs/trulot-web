The title sheet directly lists 26 dwelling areas totaling 16,370.6 square feet and ten garages totaling 5,140.0 square feet. Those values reproduce the plan's 21,510.6-square-foot new-construction subtotal. Adding the separately labeled 719.0-square-foot existing house gives 22,229.6 square feet, which conflicts by 10.0 square feet with the plan's 22,219.6-square-foot FAR numerator. Direct boundary courses on C-1 close within 0.01 foot and derive approximately 20,084.27 square feet, but the same sheet expressly states that it is not a precise boundary survey. Map 1063 was not directly reviewed. Parcel V2's approximate 20,343.182028-square-foot polygon remains diagnostic only.

The bounded civil sheets show identical existing and proposed legal descriptions and no required-dedication area or changed-boundary callout. This supports `NO_REQUIRED_DEDICATION_EVIDENCE`, not affirmative proof that no condition exists outside the reviewed corpus.

The plan's `1.10` display is numerically consistent with rounding its stated input ratio to one decimal and displaying two digits, while ordinary two-decimal rounding produces `1.11`. No explicit plan rounding convention was found. Because neither an exact Code-compatible numerator nor a legal premises denominator is sealed, no legal FAR, comparison, or margin is reported.
