## Target documents

High-value sheets are an approved site or plot plan, boundary/survey plan, grading or civil plan, foundation plan with property-line dimensions, or approved architectural site sheet. The sheet must tie to PM 17383 or another surveyed legal boundary and locate the outer edge of the building frame by adequate dimensions or offsets. Floor plans without site control, permit cards, conceptual sketches, aerials, and no-plan MEP records cannot pass the geometry gate.

## DSD Records access

Schedule a 30-minute in-person `Records Review` appointment at the City of San Diego Development Services Department Records Counter, 7650 Mission Valley Road, San Diego, CA 92108. Bring the address, APN `634-130-22-00`, PM 17383 Parcel 1, assessor year built 2010, the 2008–2012 search window, and `PMT-3276742` as a later address/APN control.

Anyone may view building plans by appointment. The City prohibits copying, tracing, sketching, photographing, or recording video during viewing. Copies require the City duplication process and written permission from the current owner and, when applicable, the signing design professional. Viewing alone is sufficient to identify and classify candidate sheets, but not to seal exact dimensions unless Records staff permits written numerical notes or an authorized copy is obtained. The operator may record project/permit identifiers, plan and sheet titles, approval dates, author names, survey/map references, geometry semantics, and copy-permission status. Exact numerical dimensions may be noted only with staff confirmation; otherwise record that the dimension exists and use the authorized duplication process.
