## Density

Tables 131-04D/E/F express RS/RX/RT density as **one dwelling unit per lot**. This is a base single-dwelling-unit limitation, not a quotient of parcel area and not a total including separately regulated accessory units. Section 113.0222(b) separately uses the zone's minimum lot area for subdivision, with downward rounding; that subdivision mechanism is captured as a reference, never executed.

Table 131-04G expresses RM density as **square feet of lot area per dwelling unit**, not dwelling units per acre or a zone-digit-derived multiplier. Footnote 1 invokes §113.0222. Its subsection (a) describes lot area divided by the base-zone square feet per dwelling unit, the 0.50 upward-rounding threshold, multi-zone summation, and rounding only once. The proposed contract preserves that documentary expression without evaluating it. Footnote 2 retains the separate affordable-housing exception; §113.0222(c) and Chapter 14 Article 3 Division 7 are explicitly outside the selected unbonused base parameter. No bonus program is implemented.

RM-5-12 footnote 36 is indispensable: within La Jolla, Pacific Beach, and Torrey Pines community-plan areas, the expression is one dwelling unit **or two guest rooms** per 1,500 square feet. Outside those areas the table's base density denominator is 1,000 square feet per dwelling unit. The community-plan and use predicates remain unresolved; the contract does not silently choose the 1,000 value. Visitor accommodations and use authorization are not determined.

Definitions §113.0103 (current PDF pp14,16,26) distinguish a legal lot, multiple dwelling units, and single dwelling units. The latter definitions exclude ADUs/JADUs; the multiple-unit definition also excludes employee housing. O-22109 clean p15 was rendered and read: the single-dwelling definition is explicit, and §113.0222(a)-(b) are **no change**. Its p16 changes subsection (c), not the base calculation. O-21836 corrected clean p11 similarly leaves (a)-(b) unchanged. Thus the July 2026 codification is not treated as an unexplained replacement for the adopted text.

## Minimum lot area

All 33 values are **base-zone minimum lot-area development standards**, under §§131.0430–131.0431. They are not universally minimum developable-parcel sizes or legal-lot tests. For single-dwelling subdivision, §113.0222(b) also uses that value as a subdivision denominator. In RM, the minimum lot area is distinct from the density denominator: for example RM-1-1 has 6,000 square feet minimum lot area but 3,000 square feet per dwelling unit as its base density expression. No subdivision or unit count is calculated.

Section 113.0237(a) contains six alternative legal-lot criteria; subsection (b) expressly permits a lot below minimum area/dimensions nevertheless to be used in compliance with its zone if it is a legal lot. Subsection (c) allows a Certificate of Compliance request. The proposal retains this exception and unresolved legal-lot predicate; it makes no determination under those criteria.

For RX/RT, §131.0441 allows lots served by an alley to use up to one-half the abutting alley width, at most 10 feet, toward minimum area, with alley area at most 10% of the minimum requirement. This is a conditional credit toward this standard, **not** an automatic expansion of legal lot area, density area, or FAR denominator. The contract preserves the two caps and the permission separately; no alley geometry is measured.
