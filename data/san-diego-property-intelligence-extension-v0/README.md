# San Diego property intelligence extension v0

Packet 77 executes the Packet 76 jurisdiction-neutral assessor, conflict, currentness, question-selection, and V4 adapters over 393,733 sealed City parcels. It commits aggregate and 25-parcel benchmark evidence only. No production source was queried, and no raw permit history, footprint polygons, owner data, or private project details are repackaged.

The decision is recon-only because no sealed Citywide permit corpus is locally available and the Citywide structure observation is the Spring 2017 historical outline layer.
